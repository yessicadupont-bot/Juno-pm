## What graders score

- **What you’re scoring:** juno's weekly dashboard draft of product adoption, engagement metrics, and insights.
- **Value proposition:** save time pulling weekly metrics across several different tools and giving insights trends or potential impacts for PM review.

## Dimensions

| Dimension | Question | Scale | 1 or No | 3 | 5 or Yes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Accuracy (deal-breaker) | is each metric accurately calculated with the correct weekly data (timeframe comparison) | Yes / No | total adoption -1% WoW adoption+200% Data from roma for anytime frame outside the 7 day window or is inaccurate or made up | n/a | total adoption 50% WoW adoption +10% Data from roma for previous 7 days (sunday - saturday) is accurate |
| Format | Are the metrics in the correct format and given constraints. | Yes / No | total adoption < 0 and >100% WoW adoption < -50% and >100% | n/a | total adoption 0-100% WoW adoption >-50% and < 100% |
| Value  | how valuable are the insights are they helpful in understanding the data? | 1 to 5 | Not helpful: The insight is unclear, generic, or unsupported. It doesn’t help me understand what the data shows. | Somewhat helpful: The insight identifies a relevant pattern or takeaway, but I still need to interpret the data myself or look for more context. | Very helpful: The insight clearly explains the key takeaway, grounds it in the data, and helps me understand why it matters or what to investigate next. |

## Calibration

- **How many answers you’ll score, and how often:** 2 answers every other week
- **Who scores them with you:** me plus one other PM
- **What happens when two of you disagree:** if scores are  no and data is inaccurate we refine and rewrite to ensure accurate data and metrics for the output

## Pass bar

every question needs to pass with a yes

**Fails outright, whatever the average:**

- Accuracy: any "no"
- hallucinations such as made up data or inaccurate data from wrong sources or timeframe
